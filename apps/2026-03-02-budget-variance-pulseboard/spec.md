# Budget Variance Pulseboard

## User story
As an FP&A lead, I need to monitor budget vs actuals by cost center, spot variance risk early, and route reforecast approvals so monthly close stays compliant.

## Features
- Scenario-driven variance tracking with search, filters, sorting, and threshold controls.
- Role-based view dimming for Finance Lead, Business Partner, and Cost Center Owner scopes.
- Approval workflow actions to request reforecast and approve variances with audit trail logging.
- CSV export for close review packs and controller sign-off.
- Top variance bar chart and policy alerts for out-of-threshold centers.

## Dave's notes
- Focused on dark-mode clarity with variance color cues and compact KPIs.
- Balanced realistic finance data across regions with approvals and audit trail behavior.
- Kept interactions lightweight but useful for FP&A cadence meetings.
