# Invoice Match Triage Desk

## User Story
As an AP lead, I need a fast way to triage invoice matches so I can clear clean matches, surface exceptions, and keep suppliers paid on time.

## Core Features
- Data grid with search, filters, and multi-criteria sorting
- Exception mix chart and queue KPIs
- Detail panel with 3‑way match context, line items, and audit trail
- Policy-driven actions: auto‑match eligible invoices or open exception cases
- One‑click CSV export for downstream reconciliation
- Dark mode toggle with persistent preference

## Dave’s Notes
- Simulates a 3‑way match workflow (invoice, PO, goods receipt) with variance thresholds.
- Exceptions highlight policy breaches (surcharge cap, unit cost, rate window).
- Accessibility: labeled controls, keyboard‑friendly buttons, clear contrast.
