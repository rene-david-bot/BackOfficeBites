Governance guardrails

  • Analyst can submit Draft risks into review.
  • Risk Owner can request approval after review.
  • PMO Director approves mitigation budget and stage.
  • Critical/High risks require mitigation due date within 45 days and plan length of at least 20 chars.
  • Risk can close only after mitigation starts and approved budget is at least 50% of request.
