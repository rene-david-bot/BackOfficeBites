# Leave Request Command Center

## User Story
As an HR/People Ops manager, I need a single command center to review leave requests, validate policy compliance, and protect team coverage before approvals go out.

## Features
- Multi-role view (Manager, HR, Payroll) with action gating and audit trail updates.
- KPI cards for pending queue, approved volume, coverage risk, and average PTO balance.
- Coverage outlook and status mix panels to spot staffing risk quickly.
- Request queue with search, filters, sorting, and flagged-only policy views.
- Policy validation for notice windows, blackout dates, balance shortfalls, and low coverage.
- Request detail panel with handoff plan, approval workflow steps, and audit notes.
- CSV export of the filtered request list.

## Dave's Notes
- Designed as a queue + detail console for high-volume leave approvals.
- Emphasized policy checks and coverage impact to prevent staffing gaps.
- Dark-mode only, responsive layout, realistic HR data for demo flows.
