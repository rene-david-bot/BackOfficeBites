# Renewal Forecast Calendar

## User story
As a sales operations manager, I need a calendar-based view of upcoming renewals so I can validate discount policies, route approvals, and keep the forecast up to date before quarter close.

## Features
- March renewal calendar with clickable account cards and quick context.
- Forecast queue with search, region/stage filters, and sortable ARR/date views.
- Role-based visibility that dims non-owned renewals while keeping policy alerts visible.
- Policy validation for discount thresholds and non-standard term exceptions.
- Approval workflow actions (request approval, approve) with audit trail logging.
- Inline editing for status, discount, and term values.
- CSV export for sharing with RevOps and finance teams.

## Dave's notes
