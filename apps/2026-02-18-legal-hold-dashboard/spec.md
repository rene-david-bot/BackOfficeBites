# Legal Hold Compliance Dashboard

## User Story
As a Legal Ops manager, I need a live dashboard of legal holds so I can monitor approvals, custodian coverage, and overdue actions before audits or regulator deadlines.

## Features
- Portfolio table with search, filter, and sort across matters, status, risk, and region.
- Role-based view selector with action gating for approvals and escalations.
- Status and risk mix charts with real-time KPI cards.
- Hold detail panel with custodian management, approval workflow, and audit trail logging.
- CSV export of the filtered hold list.
- Policy validation that blocks high-risk approvals without enough custodians.

## Dave's Notes
- Focused on a dashboard + charts pattern while still providing operational actions.
- Included audit trail and CSV export as cross-cutting compliance patterns.
- Dark-mode only, responsive layout, and realistic legal ops data for demos.
