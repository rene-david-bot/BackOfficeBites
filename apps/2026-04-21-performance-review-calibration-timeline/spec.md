# Performance Review Calibration Timeline

## User Story
As an HR business partner, I need a timeline-based calibration workspace so that managers and calibration chairs can align performance ratings, enforce policy rules, and complete approvals with a clear audit trail.

## Features
- Timeline cards for upcoming calibration reviews with workflow states and policy risk signals.
- Search, filter, and sort across employee, manager, department, and status.
- Role-based views (HRBP, Manager, Calibration Chair) with action-level permissions.
- Rating proposal form with validation for high-risk ratings.
- State machine actions: submit, approve, request rework.
- Built-in audit trail updates on every save and workflow transition.
- CSV export for filtered calibration sets.
