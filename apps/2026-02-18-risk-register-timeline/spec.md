# Risk Register Timeline

## User Story
As a planning and risk manager, I need to see upcoming mitigation milestones on a timeline so I can prioritize escalations and approvals before deadlines slip.

## Core Features
- Timeline view of active risks with next milestone dates
- Search, filter, and sort by owner, status, level, and risk score
- Approval workflow actions (request approval, approve mitigation, escalate, close)
- KPI tiles for total, high/critical, awaiting approval, and overdue
- CSV export of the filtered risk list
- Dark mode toggle with persistent preference

## Dave’s Notes
- Risk score = impact × likelihood for prioritization.
- Overdue flags appear when next review dates pass without closure.
- Audit trail updates automatically with every status change.
