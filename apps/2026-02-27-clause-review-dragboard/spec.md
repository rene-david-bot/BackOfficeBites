# Clause Review Dragboard

## User Story
As a legal operations lead, I need a fast way to triage contract clauses through review and negotiation, enforce policy checks, and capture exception approvals so that risky terms never slip through without oversight.

## Features
- Drag-and-drop lanes for Intake → Review → Negotiation → Approved
- Role-based view (Reviewer filters to assigned queue)
- Policy validation gate blocks approval without exception sign-off
- Audit trail with quick logging for decision notes
- CSV export of the filtered queue
- Quick intake form with auto-policy checks
- Search, filter, and sort across risk, clause type, and stage

## Dave's Notes
- Dark-mode only, responsive layout with Tailwind CDN
- Policy checks surface exception status and risk band inline
- Buttons for exception approval and stage promotion are wired for demo interactions
