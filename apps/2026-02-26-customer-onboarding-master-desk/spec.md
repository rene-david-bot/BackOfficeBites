# Customer Onboarding Master Desk

## User Story
As a sales ops or onboarding lead, I want a single desk to coordinate customer onboarding stages, enforce policy checks, and document approvals so that every go-live follows the same compliant workflow.

## Core Features
- Master-detail onboarding queue with search, filter, and sorting controls.
- Policy checks (KYC, security, commercial) with validation before approvals.
- Task checklist for implementation readiness with live progress tracking.
- Approval workflow actions (advance, request info, escalate risk) that update stages.
- Audit trail with timestamped notes and automated system entries.
- CSV export of the filtered queue for reporting and handoffs.

## Dave's Notes
- Kept everything dark-mode only, with high-contrast cards and controls.
- Approval action is blocked until all policy checks + tasks are complete.
- Audit trail captures every workflow decision for governance and compliance.
